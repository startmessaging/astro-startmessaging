## What Is DLT Registration and Who Needs It in India?

Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) is India's blockchain-based regulatory framework implemented under TRAI's Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TCCCPR). Designed to curb unsolicited telemarketing, smishing, and phishing attacks, DLT requires every organization sending commercial SMS to register their business identity, header tags, and message templates on a centralized telecom ledger.

### Who Must Register on DLT Portals?

Every entity sending SMS communications to Indian mobile numbers (+91) must register, including:

* **SaaS & E-Commerce Platforms:** Transactional login OTPs, order updates, and delivery alerts.
* **Fintech & Edtech Apps:** Account authentication, payment confirmations, and course reminders.
* **Healthcare & Telemedicine:** Patient appointment alerts, prescription links, and lab reports.
* **Agencies & Resellers:** Any organization sending promotional or transactional SMS on behalf of clients.

## Step-by-Step DLT Registration Walkthrough

Follow this sequential workflow to complete onboarding across Indian telecom operator portals.

### Step 1: Principal Entity (PE-ID) Registration

Choose one primary telecom DLT portal to initiate your Principal Entity onboarding (most engineering teams select Jio Trueconnect or Airtel DLT due to faster portal response times):

1. **Access the Portal:** Navigate to [Jio Trueconnect](https://trueconnect.jio.com) or [Airtel DLT Portal](https://dltregistration.airtel.in).
2. **Select Entity Type:** Choose **As a Principal Entity (PE)**.
3. **Enter Business Details:** Input your legal company name, PAN, GSTIN, and registered office address exactly as displayed on your GST certificate.
4. **Pay Registration Fee:** Pay the standard non-refundable portal fee of **₹5,900** (₹5,000 base fee + 18% GST).
5. **Upload KYC Documents:** Upload your business PAN, GST REG-06, Incorporation Certificate, and Authorised Signatory Letter.
6. **Receive PE-ID:** Operator compliance teams audit your submission within 3 to 7 working days. Upon approval, you receive a 15-digit Principal Entity ID (e.g., `100149284719283`).

### Step 2: Registering 6-Character Sender Headers (Sender IDs)

Once your PE-ID is active, apply for your organization's 6-character alphanumeric Sender Header (such as `STARTM` or `PAYVLT`):

1. Navigate to **Header Management > Add Header** inside your approved DLT portal.
2. Select Header Type: **Service Implicit** (for OTPs and transactional alerts) or **Service Explicit** (for account updates).
3. Enter your desired 6-character header string (must be uppercase letters only, e.g., `HLTAPP`).
4. Provide justification linking the 6-character tag to your registered brand name or trademark.
5. Approval takes 24 to 48 hours per header tag.

### Step 3: Submitting Content Templates

Every distinct message format sent by your backend must be pre-approved as a Content Template:

1. Navigate to **Template Management > Add Content Template**.
2. Select Message Type: **Service Implicit** (for OTPs).
3. Bind your approved Sender Header ID to the template.
4. Input the message body text using mandatory `{#var#}` variable placeholders:
   ```text
   {#var#} is your verification code for {#var#}. Valid for 5 minutes. Do not share.
   ```
5. Submit for operator review (approval takes 1 to 5 business days per template).

### Step 4: Multi-Operator Portal Synchronization

A common operational headache is that registering on Jio does not automatically authorize your headers on Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea, or BSNL. To ensure nationwide SMS deliverability, you must log into each secondary operator portal, submit your 15-digit PE-ID, and upload your approval certificate to synchronize headers across all national operator switches.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does DLT registration cost in India for a new business?**

A: DLT registration costs ₹5,900 (₹5,000 base fee + 18% GST) for initial Principal Entity (PE-ID) onboarding on your primary operator portal (Jio or Airtel). Secondary operator portals (Vi, BSNL) may charge ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 for secondary verification, bringing total first-year DLT compliance expenses to ₹8,000–₹12,000 before messaging credits.

**Q: How long does the complete DLT approval process take in India?**

A: The complete end-to-end DLT onboarding process takes between 7 and 28 business days. Initial PE-ID business KYC validation takes 3 to 7 days, 6-character Sender Header approval takes 24 to 48 hours, Content Template review takes 3 to 5 days, and multi-operator ledger synchronization adds another 5 to 10 days.

**Q: What happens if my DLT Content Template variable formatting is incorrect?**

A: If your API payload string deviates from your registered DLT Content Template (e.g., missing a variable placeholder or altering static text punctuation), telecom operator scrubbing engines drop the message silently at the carrier switch layer. The gateway receives HTTP 200 OK, but handset delivery fails.
